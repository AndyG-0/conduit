# Third-party assets

## Brand icons (`src/assets/logos/`)

Streaming-service brand marks used to identify each service's tile. These
are the vendors' own trademarks; inclusion here is to identify the linked
service, not a claim of affiliation with or endorsement by the trademark
owner.

- `netflix.svg`, `hbomax.svg`, `tubi.svg`, `paramountplus.svg`,
  `youtubetv.svg`, `appletv.svg` — from [simple-icons](https://simpleicons.org/)
  (CC0-1.0). simple-icons' own guidelines note these marks are provided for
  the purpose of representing the linked brand/service.
- `hulu.svg`, `disney-plus.svg`, `peacock.svg`, `amazon-prime-video.svg` —
  from [selfhst/icons](https://github.com/selfhst/icons) (CC-BY-4.0).
  Attribution: icons by [selfhst](https://github.com/selfhst), licensed
  under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

ESPN and Sling TV aren't present in either icon set as of 2026-09-24; those
tiles fall back to a generated monogram badge (see `src/icons.ts`) until a
legitimately-licensed mark is found.
